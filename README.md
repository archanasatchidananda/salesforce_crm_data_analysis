# CRM Analytics – BI Capstone Project

**CRM Analytics** is a Business Intelligence (BI) capstone project aimed at analyzing customer relationship management data to drive actionable insights. The project uses a combination of **Excel, Tableau, Power BI, and SQL** to uncover sales performance, lead conversion trends, and opportunity management effectiveness.

## Project Summary

The main goal of the project was to **analyze and optimize field force effectiveness** using CRM data. By understanding trends in lead generation, sales opportunities, and customer conversion, businesses can make more strategic decisions to improve sales outcomes and operational efficiency.

### Business Objective

To design a **sustainable and scalable analysis system** that helps organizations:
- Track and improve sales performance
- Increase lead conversion rates
- Forecast revenue more accurately
- Identify high-performing sectors and sources

## Tools & Technologies Used
 - Excel
 - Tableau
 - Power BI
 - MySQL

## Dataset Overview

The analysis was based on **five interrelated datasets**, simulating real-world CRM tables:

| Table Name             | Description                          | Records |
|------------------------|--------------------------------------|---------|
| Account                | Customer and account details         | 3,052   |
| Lead                   | Raw leads information                | 10,000  |
| Opportunity            | Sales opportunities and outcomes     | 4,647   |
| Opportunity Product    | Products linked to opportunities     | 10,000  |
| User                   | Sales representative/user data       | 100     |

These datasets were cleaned, merged, and transformed for further analysis and dashboarding.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The project tracked various KPIs to measure the success of sales efforts and lead management.

### 🔹 Lead-Related KPIs
- **Total Leads**: Volume of raw leads in the system
- **Converted Leads**: Number of leads that turned into paying customers
- **Conversion Rate**
- **Lead by Source/Industry/Stage**: Breakdowns to identify where the best leads come from and what stages need improvement

### 🔹 Opportunity-Related KPIs
- **Expected Amount**: Predicted revenue from open opportunities
- **Active Opportunities**: Total number of current (open) opportunities
- **Win Rate / Loss Rate**

### 🔹 Trend Analysis KPIs
- **Expected vs Forecast Revenue**: Running total over time
- **Active vs Total Opportunities**: Insights into sales pipeline health
- **Closed Won vs Total / Closed**: Success rate and deal closure quality
- **Opportunities by Industry / Type**: Strategic breakdown for segmentation


## Key Insights & Takeaways

1. **2020** was the year with the **most converted accounts**, indicating peak performance.
2. The **Insight Sales** and **Safety & Security** sectors produced the **highest lead volumes**.
3. **2021** recorded the **most active opportunities**, showing improved engagement.
4. **Year-on-year growth** in sales indicates strong business scalability.
5. Focused improvements in **lead sources** and **conversion strategies** could unlock further growth potential.
